## A little about how I work

Curiosity is at the center of how I work. I like understanding how people think, where workflows break down, and which details can make a product easier to use.

I work across research, user flows, interaction design, prototyping, and design systems—collaborating with stakeholders and developers to bring ideas into implementation.


## What I bring to a project

- Interaction design and prototyping
- Design systems and scalable UI
- Design-to-development collaboration
- Data-informed research and UX quality review
